# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and respect for differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and receiving constructive technical feedback graciously.
- Focusing discussions on what is best for users and the community.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
trolling, threats, publishing another person's private information, or other
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and whenever someone is representing the
project in public. It applies to maintainers, contributors, and participants
alike.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. If contacting a
particular maintainer would be inappropriate, contact another maintainer. All
reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation for behavior that violates these standards. Enforcement
decisions will consider the context, severity, and impact of the behavior.
